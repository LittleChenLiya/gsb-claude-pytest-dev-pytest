Added the ``--explain-marks`` command-line option: after collection, pytest prints a read-only report explaining, for every collected item, each effective marker occurrence with its origin (module, class, parametrize or function) and its real priority. Same-name markers are listed individually in their original order, each parametrized item keeps its own parameter-level marks, unregistered markers are reported in a separate diagnostics section, and the report never modifies items or triggers fixtures.
